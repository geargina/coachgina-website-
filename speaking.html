About the speaker

Geargina Tan is COO and co-founder of WTFox.ai, a Singapore AI company, where agents run operations and she owns the governance that keeps them accountable. She also works as a fractional compliance consultant, taking a SaaS company through SOC 2, ISO 27001, and GDPR, which gives her a practical view of where governance falls behind deployment.

Before AI she built a career across sales, operations, and leadership at Amazon, Jewel Changi Airport, and Resorts World Sentosa (Universal Studios Singapore). Today she writes and speaks as Coach Gina, teaching business leaders across Southeast Asia how to use AI rather than talk about it, and runs workshops for founders, executives, and their teams.

Booking questions

Does Geargina charge a speaking fee?

Yes for corporate and commercial events. Community and non-profit rooms are discussed case by case. Tell me the audience, the date and the format on the contact form, and I'll come back with a number that fits the room. Travel outside Singapore is quoted separately, and I'm happy to say so up front if the budget isn't there.

What formats does she offer?

Three shapes, mostly. A talk of 20 to 45 min, a panel seat, or a hands-on workshop that runs from 2 hrs to a full day. I teach on Claude and ChatGPT, whichever your team already pays for. For anything hands-on, everyone brings a laptop, because people only remember the thing they did themselves.

Does she speak online?

Yes, and often. Recent online sessions have had people joining from Indonesia and the Philippines alongside the Singapore crowd, so a regional audience is normal rather than a special case. Online works well for a talk or a 2 hr session. For a full-day workshop I'd rather be in the room with you, because that's when the questions get good.

What does the organiser need to provide?

A short brief on who's in the room and what they do, so I can pitch it at the right level. A screen and audio I can plug into. Wifi that holds up if the session is hands-on, since everyone will be logged into an AI tool at once. And the run of show about a week out.

How far ahead should I book?

A few weeks for a talk, longer for a workshop built around your team's own documents. Q4 2026 dates are being booked now. If your date is tighter than that, ask anyway, because a panel or a short talk can usually be made to work. A workshop is the one thing I won't rush, since the prep is what makes it useful.
